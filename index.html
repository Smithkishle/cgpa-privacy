Privacy Policy

CGPA - Grade Calculator · Effective date: November 18, 2026

In short: the app does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

To work as a calculator, the app saves a small amount of data in its private storage on your device:

  • The semesters you add on the CGPA screen (semester names, SGPA values and credit hours).
  • The grade scale you set on the Grade List screen, if you change it.

This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. The courses and marks you type on the SGPA screen are used only to show your result and are not saved.

What the app does not collect

  • No account or login is required, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.

Internet and third parties

The app works fully offline and does not send your data to any server. No third-party service receives information from the app. Google Play, where you download the app, may collect its own information under Google’s privacy policy; the app itself does not.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
